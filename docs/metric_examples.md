## Semi-additivity check

`active_accounts` is a stock, so its measure is semi-additive over `statement_month` (`non_additive_dimension`, `window_choice: max`). At quarter grain it must return the last month's count, not the sum of three months.

| | active_accounts |
|---|---|
| Q4 2025 (quarter grain) | 46,179 |
| Dec 2025 (month grain) | 46,179 |
| Oct + Nov + Dec 2025 (what a naive sum would return) | 135,425 |

Result: **PASS** - Q4 2025 equals December 2025.
